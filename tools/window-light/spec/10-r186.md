## 2. (a) What r170 to r186 gives this scene

### 2.1 Renderer: WebGPURenderer and its WebGL2 fallback

- `new WebGPURenderer()` from `three/webgpu`. Constructor sets `WebGPUBackend` and a `getFallback` that returns `WebGLBackend` and warns "WebGPU is not available, running under WebGL2 backend." (`src/renderers/webgpu/WebGPURenderer.js:57-71`, VERIFIED). The fallback fires when `backend.init()` throws, i.e. no `navigator.gpu` or `requestAdapter()` returns null (`src/renderers/common/Renderer.js:796-820`, VERIFIED). `forceWebGL: true` forces WebGL2 (useful for testing the low tier).
- Since r181 you must `await renderer.init()` (or use `setAnimationLoop`) before sync calls; `renderAsync()` is deprecated (Migration Guide 180 to 181, VERIFIED).
- The adapter is requested with `featureLevel: 'compatibility'` (`WebGPUBackend.js:215-218`); if the device lacks `core-features-and-limits` the renderer runs in compatibility mode and forces `_samples = 0`, i.e. **no MSAA** (`WebGPUBackend.js:258-262`, VERIFIED; release r183 "Turn off antialiasing in compat mode"). Consequence: rely on TRAA or SMAA, not `antialias: true` (INFERRED).
- Default options that matter: `alpha = true` (premultiplied canvas), `antialias = false`, `outputBufferType = HalfFloatType` (float16 internal buffers) (`WebGPURenderer.js:33-45` JSDoc, VERIFIED). r185 migration: "configure an opaque background ... Only use transparent background or clear colors if your application has to blend with the HTML background" (VERIFIED). For this scene that is exactly the DOM-on-monitor case (see 2.10).
- Only NodeMaterials render. `StandardNodeLibrary.js:64-76` maps the classic materials (Standard, Physical, Basic, Lambert, Phong, Toon, Line, Points, Sprite, Shadow...) to node equivalents; there is no mapping for `ShaderMaterial`/`RawShaderMaterial`, and `NodeBuilder.js:3158-3163` logs `Material "ShaderMaterial" is not compatible.` (VERIFIED). **The existing Living Sky GLSL cannot be dropped into a WebGPURenderer scene as a ShaderMaterial.** See 2.8.
- The reverse bridge (r184 "WebGLRenderer: Add NodeMaterial compatibility layer") lives in `examples/jsm/tsl/WebGLNodesHandler.js`; its own header (lines 29-36) lists: no VSM, no MRT, no transmission, **no WebGPU post-processing stack**, no storage textures (VERIFIED). Not a path for this scene.
- New in r186: `DirectRenderPipeline` (`src/renderers/common/DirectRenderPipeline.js`) applies output transform inside materials, skipping the intermediate framebuffer, but "is not compatible with materials that sample the framebuffer, such as transmissive materials" (VERIFIED). Not for us (we need transmission and post).

### 2.2 TSL and the post-processing pipeline

- `PostProcessing` was renamed `RenderPipeline` in r183 (Migration 182 to 183, VERIFIED). Pattern: `const rp = new RenderPipeline(renderer); const scenePass = pass(scene, camera); scenePass.setMRT(mrt({ output, emissive, normal: ..., velocity })); rp.outputNode = ...; rp.render();`.
- `RenderPipeline.outputColorTransform = true` by default (`RenderPipeline.js:75`) and wraps the final node in `renderOutput(outputNode, toneMapping, outputColorSpace)` (`:192-194`) (VERIFIED). So tone mapping applies once to the **whole frame**, including anything drawn on the monitor mesh (INFERRED consequence: brand colours of the portfolio would be tone-mapped if the screen is a texture inside the pipeline).
- r186 TSL additions relevant here (release notes, VERIFIED): `softParticles()` (#33887), `billboarding()` gains `horizontalRotation` (#34197), individual `lighting` per `pass()` (#34051), `OnBeforeRenderPipeline`/`OnAfterRenderPipeline` (#34025), `RTTNode` `autoUpdate` + `resolutionScale` (#34307), `compileComputeAsync()` (#32551). r184: TSL compile 3.0x faster (#33120), `OnFrameUpdate` (#33356). r183: `exponentialHeightFogFactor()` (#32659).
- TSL exports I checked exist in `build/three.tsl.js`: `billboarding, exponentialHeightFogFactor, densityFogFactor, rangeFogFactor, fog, mx_noise_float, mx_fractal_noise_float, mx_worley_noise_float, hash, time, deltaTime, positionPrevious, instanceIndex, rtt, pass, mrt, emissive, velocity, normalView, viewportSharedTexture, viewportMipTexture, screenUV, interleavedGradientNoise, renderOutput, agxToneMapping, neutralToneMapping, triNoise3D, builtinGIContext, convertToTexture` (VERIFIED by grep). `bayer16` is in `three/addons/tsl/math/Bayer.js`.

### 2.3 Volumetric light and haze

- `VolumeNodeMaterial` (`src/materials/nodes/VolumeNodeMaterial.js`) with `steps`, `offsetNode` (dither), `scatteringNode(positionRay)`; lights opt in via layers. `webgpu_volume_lighting.html:133-194` drives it with a **shadowed SpotLight (with `spotLight.map`)** and a PointLight, renders the volume in a separate `pass()` at `setResolutionScale(.25)`, blurs with `gaussianBlur`, and adds it to the scene pass (VERIFIED). This is the right primitive for the lamp cone in haze and the dust in it. `webgpu_volume_lighting_traa` does the same with TRAA and `interleavedGradientNoise` instead of blur; `webgpu_volume_lighting_rectarea` for area lights (VERIFIED titles and imports).
- r186 added `webgpu_postprocessing_fog` (#34319): a raymarched volumetric fog **post pass** using scene depth, a `SunLight` with shadows, `rtt(volumetricFogPass(), ..., { resolutionScale })` at 0.4 and `gaussianBlur` (`webgpu_postprocessing_fog.html:175-185, 299, 350-460`, VERIFIED). Template for outdoor haze and sun shafts through the grille.
- r185: volumetric fire example and `VolumetricLightingModel` fixes (#33848, #33896). `webgpu_custom_fog_scattering` (improved in r185 and r186) fakes forward scattering by mixing a `gaussianBlur`red scene colour by fog density (lines 200-211) (VERIFIED).
- Fog nodes: `fog(color, densityFogFactor(d))`, `rangeFogFactor`, `exponentialHeightFogFactor` (r171 and r183 migration notes, VERIFIED exports).

### 2.4 Lights and shadows

- `SpotLight(color, intensity, distance, angle = PI/3, penumbra = 0, decay = 2)`; **`angle` is the half-angle** ("Maximum angle of light dispersion from its direction whose upper bound is Math.PI/2", `src/lights/SpotLight.js:35,80`) and `spotLight.map` projects a texture (gobo) (VERIFIED). So "37 degrees" as a full cone is `angle = 0.323 rad`, as a half-angle `0.646 rad`.
- `IESSpotLight` and `ProjectorLight` (`src/lights/webgpu/`): "Can only be used with WebGPURenderer" (either backend) (VERIFIED). IES gives a real dome-lamp photometric falloff if we have or author a profile.
- `RectAreaLight`: "There is no shadow support" (`src/lights/RectAreaLight.js:10`, VERIFIED). Good for window skylight fill and monitor glow, not for bar shadows.
- `SunLight` (r186, addons: `examples/jsm/lights/SunLight.js`): directional light with cascaded shadow maps via `SunLightShadow`, direction defined by position (no target); with WebGPURenderer you must call `renderer.library.addLight(SunLightNode, SunLight)` (file header, VERIFIED); r186 reduced cascades to 2. For a room-sized scene a plain `DirectionalLight` with a tight ortho shadow camera is enough (INFERRED).
- Shadow filtering: **`PCFSoftShadowMap` removed in r186** for both renderers; `PCFShadowMap` is now soft (Vogel disk, `shadow.radius` scales it: `ShadowFilterNode.js:48-79`); `VSMShadowMap` remains (VERIFIED). r183: shadows improved, reduce old bias values (Migration 182 to 183).
- r184 `ColorUtils.setKelvin(color, kelvin)` in `examples/jsm/utils/ColorUtils.js:23` (Tanner Helland approximation, clamps 1000 to 40000 K) (VERIFIED). Useful for the lamp bulb temperature and the sun colour by elevation.

### 2.5 Global illumination and probes

- `LightProbeGrid` (WebGPU version, r186; the WebGL one is renamed `LightProbeGridWebGL`): 3D grid of L2 SH irradiance probes stored in a `RenderTarget3D` atlas, added to the scene like a light; `bake(renderer, scene, { start, count, ... })` supports **incremental baking** (r186 #34486) and indirect bounces (r185) (`examples/jsm/lighting/LightProbeGrid.js:269-454`, VERIFIED). Fits a static room whose light changes slowly with time of day: re-bake a slice per frame when the sun moves (INFERRED).
- `VXGINode` / `vxgi()` (r186, `examples/jsm/lighting/vxgi/`): voxel cone traced diffuse GI + AO, dynamic lights without rebake, geometry should stay static; "can only be used with WebGPURenderer and a WebGPU backend" (`VXGINode.js:41`, VERIFIED). Desktop WebGPU enhancement only.
- `SSGINode` (screen space, both backends) sits between (see 2.2).
- PMREM: r186 switched the PMREM blur to a spiral blur on both renderers (#32367); **r187 (dev) will rebuild PMREM on cube render targets and remove `CubeUVReflectionMapping`**, "materials will look slightly different" (Migration 186 to 187, VERIFIED). Pinning 0.186.1 avoids that shift mid-build.
- r181 and r186 lighting-model changes: better energy conservation, rough materials slightly brighter (Migration 180 to 181; r186 #33983, #33985) (VERIFIED). Affects the DJ console look (section 4).

### 2.6 Glass, paint, wood

- Transmission in node materials samples `viewportMipTexture()` (`src/nodes/functions/PhysicalLightingModel.js:19,70`, VERIFIED): rough transmission = blurred background, which is what frosted panes look like. Transmission is excluded by `OITPassNode` and `DirectRenderPipeline` (VERIFIED from their headers).
- `backdropNode` + `viewportSharedTexture()` + `hashBlur()` (`webgpu_backdrop_area.html:93-113`) give cheaper frosted or dusty glass without full transmission (VERIFIED).
- Glossy paint on the frame: `MeshPhysicalNodeMaterial` with `clearcoat` and `clearcoatRoughness` (standard). `WoodNodeMaterial` (addons) has presets `teak, walnut, ... mahogany` and finishes `raw, matte, semigloss, gloss` (`WoodNodeMaterial.js:277-278`, VERIFIED): usable for the desk or any bare wood; the frame is painted, so grain would only show through as subtle normal detail (INFERRED from the photos).
- r186 retroreflectivity on MeshPhysicalMaterial (#33949) is irrelevant here.

### 2.7 Foliage, wind, particles

- **There is no wind or vegetation-sway helper anywhere in r186 core or addons** (grep for `wind`/`sway` over `src` and `examples/jsm` finds only unrelated comments) (VERIFIED). Wind is our own TSL `material.positionNode`.
- `TreeGenerator` (addons, r185/r186) "produces branches only; add foliage as a separate layer", deterministic by `seed`, fluent `setSeed().setLevels()...build()` (`examples/jsm/generators/TreeGenerator.js` header). `ForestGenerator` plants distorted icosphere "trees" in one InstancedMesh (VERIFIED). Good for the distant stand of larger trees, not for the near tree's leaves (INFERRED).
- Leaves: `InstancedMesh` (per-instance matrices, colours) or `BatchedMesh` (r183 per-instance opacity, wireframe; r186 `positionPrevious` support) with `alphaHash` (`webgpu_materials_alphahash`) + TRAA for soft cut-outs (INFERRED choice). `MeshSSSNodeMaterial` (experimental, `thicknessColorNode`, `thicknessDistortionNode`, `thicknessPowerNode`, `thicknessScaleNode`) gives backlit leaf translucency, which is the whole look of golden hour through leaves (VERIFIED props, INFERRED use).
- Motion vectors: velocity uses `positionPrevious` (`src/nodes/accessors/Position.js:54`), which instancing, batching and skinning update with `positionPrevious.assign(...)` (`Instance.js:229`, `Skinning.js:272`) (VERIFIED). A wind offset added in `positionNode` is **not** in `positionPrevious`, so TRAA would see the whole sway as motion and smear or ghost leaves unless we also assign the previous-frame wind offset to `positionPrevious` (INFERRED from the code path; this is the single most likely foliage bug).
- Rain and snow: `webgpu_compute_particles_rain` / `_snow` (compute + `instancedArray` + `billboarding`) (VERIFIED imports). On the WebGL2 fallback, compute runs through transform feedback (`WebGLBackend.js:918-953`), so it degrades rather than disappears (VERIFIED code path, INFERRED performance).
- Dust motes in the lamp beam: `softParticles()` (r186) as in `webgpu_particles_soft` (VERIFIED import).

### 2.8 Sky

- `SkyMesh` (WebGPU only; `Sky` is WebGL only) now has procedural clouds (r183), "more realistic clouds" and slower default speed (r186), optional sun disc (r184), and the `up` uniform removed (r186). Uniforms: `turbidity, rayleigh, mieCoefficient, mieDirectionalG, sunPosition, cloudScale, cloudSpeed, cloudCoverage, cloudDensity, cloudElevation, showSunDisc` (`examples/jsm/objects/SkyMesh.js:58-128`) (VERIFIED). r183 removed a legacy gamma curve so old Sky looks cannot be reproduced (Migration 182 to 183).
- The portfolio's own Living Sky is a 724-line GLSL ES 1.0 fragment shader (`src/shaders/washes.js:514` onward) with 28 `u_*` uniforms including `u_wind, u_gust, u_sunElev, u_sunAz, u_moonph, u_stars, u_precip, u_fog, u_haze`, written in **screen space** (`u_resolution`, `gl_FragCoord`) and rendered by a raw WebGL1 context in `src/ShaderCanvas.jsx:80` (VERIFIED). `u_sunAz` is a frame x position (`weather.js:319`, `s.frameX`), not a world azimuth (VERIFIED).
- Ways to get it into the scene: (1) port to TSL so it runs in both backends and inside the pipeline (bloom, haze and glass all see it), with the r186 transpiler (`examples/jsm/transpiler/GLSLDecoder.js` + `TSLEncoder.js`, example `webgpu_tsl_transpiler`; r186 added preprocessor directive, macro and array support #34206, #34208) as the first pass and hand cleanup after (INFERRED effort: the decoder polyfills `gl_FragCoord` at `GLSLDecoder.js:260`, but a 724-line shader will need fixes); (2) keep ShaderCanvas and upload its canvas each frame as a `CanvasTexture` onto a sky card behind the window (fast to ship, a second GL context and a per-frame copy) (INFERRED). Recommendation in section 5.

### 2.10 Live DOM on the monitor

- **`HTMLTexture`** (r184, updated r185): a texture from a live HTML element using the WICG HTML-in-Canvas API (`src/textures/HTMLTexture.js`). WebGL path: sets `layoutsubtree` on the canvas, appends the element as a child, listens to `onpaint`, uploads with `texElementImage2D` (two signatures: "Chrome 150+" and "Chrome 138 - 149", `src/renderers/webgl/WebGLTextures.js:1257-1314`); WebGPU path uses `device.queue.copyElementImageToTexture` (`WebGPUTextureUtils.js:714-754`); the WebGL2 fallback of WebGPURenderer handles it too (`webgl-fallback/utils/WebGLTextureUtils.js:667-685`) (VERIFIED). `InteractionManager` (addons, `examples/jsm/interaction/InteractionManager.js`) keeps each element under its mesh with a CSS `matrix3d`, so the browser dispatches real pointer events to it (VERIFIED header). Example: `webgpu_materials_texture_html` (VERIFIED), which falls back to the third-party polyfill `three-html-render/polyfill` (npm `three-html-render@0.1.2`, VERIFIED on npm; fidelity unknown, INFERRED risky).
- Availability of the underlying API: Chrome-only **origin trial**, desktop and Android first milestone 148, last 150, extension 1 to M154, extension 2 to M160 (blink-dev "Intent to Extend Experiment: HTML-in-canvas", 2026-09-07); Mozilla and WebKit "No signal"; locally needs `chrome://flags/#canvas-draw-element`; does not render cross-origin iframes; scroll and animation inside the element are tied to JS (Chrome for Developers blog) (VERIFIED as fetched). **Not shippable as the primary path for a portfolio in Sept 2026.**
- `CSS3DRenderer` + `CSS3DObject` (addons) place real DOM with CSS 3D transforms; `css3d_mixed.html:49-95` layers a transparent WebGL canvas (`pointerEvents: 'none'`) over the CSS3D layer and punches a hole with a `MeshBasicMaterial({ blending: NoBlending, opacity: 0, premultipliedAlpha: true })` plane so the DOM shows through, framed by 3D geometry (VERIFIED; that example uses WebGLRenderer, the same trick needs `alpha: true` on WebGPURenderer, which is its default).
- `HTMLMesh` (`examples/jsm/interactive/HTMLMesh.js`) re-rasterises DOM to a canvas itself (r186 improved form controls); limited CSS support (INFERRED from history, not re-tested).

## 5. (d) Recommended stack for this scene

### 5.1 The stack

1. **three 0.186.1, `WebGPURenderer` with automatic WebGL2 fallback**, one scene module lazy-loaded behind today's Living Sky. Feature-gate with `renderer.backend.isWebGPUBackend` (pattern used in `SSGINode.js:414`). Reasons: it is the only renderer that gets the node post stack (volumetrics, GTAO/SSAO, SSGI, TRAA/TAAU, DOF, OIT, selective bloom via MRT), IES and projector spots, HTMLTexture on WebGPU, HDR output, compute particles, and it degrades to WebGL2 instead of failing (VERIFIED capabilities, INFERRED recommendation).
2. **Canvas**: `antialias: false`, `outputBufferType: HalfFloatType` (default), `toneMapping = AgXToneMapping` for the room (keeps the saturated dusk sky and the warm lamp from skewing hue) or `NeutralToneMapping` if A/B against the photos prefers it; `alpha: true` only because the DOM hand-off needs the hole (2.10). SDR output for v1 (HDR is a question).
3. **Pass graph** (desktop WebGPU tier, INFERRED):
   `scenePass = pass(scene, camera)` with `mrt({ output, emissive, normal: packNormalToRGB(normalView), velocity })`
   -> AO: `ssao()` or `ao()` (or baked AO if the room is baked)
   -> lamp volume: separate `pass()` on a volumetric layer with `VolumeNodeMaterial` (lamp SpotLight + sun), `setResolutionScale(0.25 to 0.5)`, blurred, added
   -> outdoor haze: height fog nodes on the outside geometry, or the r186 post fog pattern
   -> `bloom(emissivePass)` selective, optional `lensflare(bloom)` for the sun
   -> `dof(..., scenePass.getViewZNode(), focusDistance, ...)` animated by scroll (window to monitor rack focus)
   -> `traa(...)` (or `taau(...)` with the scene pass at 0.67 to 0.75 scale on DPR 2)
   -> `film` + light `chromaticAberration` + `lut3D` grade
   -> `renderOutput` (automatic, `outputColorTransform`).
4. **Lights**: lamp = `SpotLight` (or `IESSpotLight` with a dome-reflector profile) with 2048 shadow map, `PCFShadowMap`, `shadow.radius` for softness, colour from `setKelvin`; sun = `DirectionalLight` driven by `sunUniforms()` from `src/lib/weather.js:314` (same maths the Living Sky uses), tight ortho shadow camera for bar and grille shadows; window skylight fill = `RectAreaLight` (no shadows) tinted from the sky; monitor glow = `RectAreaLight` or emissive + bloom.
5. **GI**: `LightProbeGrid` (both backends) re-baked incrementally as time of day moves; `vxgi()` only as an optional desktop WebGPU upgrade; `scene.environment` = PMREM of the room + sky, refreshed on time-of-day change, for clearcoat paint and glass reflections.
6. **Glass**: thin dielectric panes with a dust/smudge texture driving roughness and a faint diffuse scatter lit by the lamp; rough transmission (`viewportMipTexture`) or `backdropNode` + `hashBlur` for the frosted panes; Fresnel reflections that take over at dusk and night (the lamp and monitor reflected in the window). Rain streaks and drops on glass as a separate normal-map layer.
7. **Sky**: port Living Sky to TSL (both backends, inside the pipeline) keeping the exact `u_*` names so `skyUniforms()`/`sunUniforms()` drive it unchanged; render it with `rtt(..., { resolutionScale })` onto a far sky card seen through the window. Ship phase 1 with the CanvasTexture bridge if the port takes long. `SkyMesh` only as a physical reference for sun colour and ambient.
8. **Trees**: near tree = branches (hand-modelled glTF or `TreeGenerator`) + leaf cards as `InstancedMesh` with `alphaHash`, `MeshSSSNodeMaterial` for backlit translucency, wind in `positionNode` (TSL noise, two frequencies: trunk sway and leaf flutter, gust envelope) **with `positionPrevious` fed the previous-frame offset** for TRAA; distant trees = `billboarding()` impostors or `ForestGenerator`-style blobs in fog.
9. **Weather input**: extend the Open-Meteo `current=` list in `src/lib/weather.js:231` with `wind_direction_10m` and `wind_gusts_10m` (today only `wind_speed_10m` is fetched, VERIFIED), plus `precipitation`/`rain` for droplet intensity if wanted.
10. **DOM**: DOM hand-off (question 1): live portfolio stays real DOM; while the camera travels the screen shows a texture (HTMLTexture where the origin trial is present, otherwise a captured still of the page top); when the camera parks, a CSS3D/punch-through hole shows the real DOM exactly in the screen rect, with glass glare and lamp reflection drawn over it at low alpha by the canvas. This keeps text crisp, accessible and un-tone-mapped (INFERRED).
11. **Run-time hygiene**: `await renderer.init()`, `await renderer.compileAsync(scene, camera)` before revealing, Lenis progress (already a dependency) drives a spline camera, render on demand when idle, drop to a low rate or pause once the reader is inside the monitor, `THREE.Timer` not `Clock`.
12. **Low tier** (WebGL2 fallback or slow GPU, INFERRED): same scene, no SSGI/VXGI/GTAO (baked AO), volumetric at 0.25 scale and 6 steps, SMAA instead of TRAA, fewer leaves, DPR 1.5; if frame time still misses budget, keep the plain Living Sky and skip the room.

## 6. Scene-specific gotchas found while reading

1. Tone mapping covers the whole frame in `RenderPipeline` (`RenderPipeline.js:192-194`): portfolio colours on a monitor texture will shift under AgX. Reason enough to hand off to real DOM (VERIFIED mechanism, INFERRED impact).
2. Wind in `positionNode` without `positionPrevious` breaks TRAA and motion blur on leaves (2.7).
3. `GodraysNode` and `SSSNode` do not support spot lights; the lamp beam needs `VolumeNodeMaterial` (2.2, 2.3).
4. `RectAreaLight` has no shadows; bar and grille shadows must come from the sun and the lamp shadow maps (2.4).
5. `OITPassNode` drops transmissive materials; frosted panes that use transmission must stay out of OIT (2.2).
6. No MSAA on WebGPU compatibility devices; thin iron bars and window mullions alias badly without TRAA/TAAU/SMAA (2.1).
7. The Living Sky is screen-space and its `u_sunAz` is a frame coordinate; as a sky card behind a window seen from a moving camera it will need the card to be far and large, or a direction-based port (2.8, INFERRED).
8. HTMLTexture is an origin-trial API; do not make it load-bearing (2.10).
9. r187 will change PMREM (cube targets, `CubeUVReflectionMapping` removed); pin `^0.186.1` for this build and budget a look check when moving to 187 (2.5).
10. `SunLight` needs `renderer.library.addLight(SunLightNode, SunLight)` on WebGPURenderer, easy to miss (2.4).
