Ekam · Dehradun · content plan v1 · 25 Jul 2026

Six slots, six formats,
one voice

A system for producing platform-native content fast, without re-deciding the voice every time. It works because of one reduction: every clinical topic collapses into the same six slots — and every platform format is just a different arrangement of those six. Fill the slots once per topic, and the topic can be shipped to Instagram, X and Reddit without rewriting the thinking.

Layer 1

The spine

Six slots. Every topic gets reduced to these, once, by whoever holds the clinical knowledge. This is the only step that needs Dr. Richa — everything downstream is assembly. The order below is the order of trust: you earn attention with a true sentence, remove a wrong belief, explain the mechanism, admit the limit, then say what to do. Warmth goes last so it reads as care rather than as a sales close.

1 · Hook
The truest sharp sentence available.Never a curiosity gap. If it needs a "wait for it," it isn't the hook — it's bait, and it breaks the register.
2 · Myth
What she has already been told, that is wrong.Correct the belief, never the person holding it. Most patients arrive pre-loaded with bad advice they were given in good faith.
3 · Mechanism
What is physically happening, in plain words. 2–4 beats.The load-bearing slot. One beat per frame or per post — never two mechanisms in one breath.
4 · Limit
What this does not fix, or cannot promise.The trust slot, and the one everyone wants to cut. It's why the rest is believed. "Good protection, not a guarantee."
5 · Next
The actual next action, and the real stakes if ignored.State consequence as fact, never as fear. "This raises your risk of X" — not "don't let this destroy your future."
6 · Warmth
The non-scolding close.Specific, not generic. "We'll explain it again, differently" beats "don't worry!" — and never apologise for what she came in with.

The four-question filter — run before anything ships

01
Does it moralize a symptom anywhere, even by implication?
02
Does it euphemize past the point of clarity?
03
Does it use fear or shock to drive the booking?
04
Does the discretion promise read like an apology for why she's here?

Any yes — rewrite. And one addition specific to generating at speed: if a sentence could have been written about any clinic, cut it. Volume is the enemy of voice, and generic filler is the first thing that creeps in when you're producing fast.

Using it, and its limits

The builder produces a scaffold, not a finished post. It guarantees structure, order and voice — it cannot supply the clinical judgment in slot 3, and it shouldn't. Treat the output as the draft you edit, and keep two rules alongside it: pricing and dosing get verified against the front desk before anything goes public, and anything compressed from a longer draft gets read back by Dr. Richa as a published sentence, not as a summary of her own writing.

Extending it is deliberately cheap. A new topic is six slots. A new platform is one arrangement of those six, plus its own rules — the shape of the system doesn't change when a channel is added, which is the whole point of building it this way rather than as a library of finished posts.
