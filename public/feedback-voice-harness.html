Feedback layer, voice audition

Four candidate voices for the portfolio's sound vocabulary, across the seven events the site actually has. Pick one voice, or mix per event. Whatever you choose gets written into the spec.

Everything here is synthesized live with Web Audio. No audio files, nothing to load, nothing to license. The whole layer is a few hundred bytes of code rather than a folder of samples, which is the only reason sound is defensible on a portfolio at all.
